04 Service Process

Four steps, no surprises.

The same sequence every time — so you always know which step you're on and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Describe the problem

    Tell us what you're hearing, feeling, or seeing — in your own words. Online request or phone call, both reach the same shop.

  2. 02

    Inspect and explain

    We inspect the relevant systems, document what we find, and walk you through it in plain language.

  3. 03

    Approve the work

    You receive a written recommendation first. Nothing gets started until you say yes.

  4. 04

    Get back on the road

    Work is completed, findings are kept for your records, and we go over what to watch as you drive away.

05 What to Expect

No reviews here. Just the process.

This shop is a fictional demo, so there are no customer quotes on this page. What we can show you is exactly how a visit works.

  1. You describe the symptom in your own words

    "Grinding when I brake" is enough to get started — no forms full of jargon.

  2. The shop explains what it found

    Findings are documented and shown to you — photos, measurements, and what they actually mean.

  3. You receive a written recommendation before work starts

    The proposed work arrives in writing first, so you can read it, compare it, or ask about any line.

  4. You decide what happens next

    Approve it, postpone it, or ask more questions. The car doesn't move without your yes.

06 About the Shop

A shop built around one rule.

Northstar Auto Care is a fictional independent shop in Seattle, built for this portfolio to show how a local repair business can explain itself online.

Most repair sites talk about horsepower and trust. This one starts from the only thing that actually matters when your car is in someone else's bay: nothing happens without your approval.
